KernelX is a project about having freedom in school. When you are bored in school and have no way out, what are you supposed to do? Sit and do nothing? With this project, you can have peace of mind, not worrying that you will be bored to death at school. But wait, how will these stay online? The answer to that question is simple; these cannot get blocked. As long as you have them downloaded, you are good to go. No worrying about blockers or any of that stuff. You will always have the freedom to play games at school. Now, go download these games and have fun. You're welcome!
